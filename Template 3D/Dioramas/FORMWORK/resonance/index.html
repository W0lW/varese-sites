On sound

Sound is the fastest sense. It reaches you before you decide to listen.

We treat a room, a brand, or a film the way a composer treats a score — as a system of tensions waiting to resolve. Every project begins in silence, then earns each frequency it adds. Nothing is placed until it has a reason to sound.

Our work lives in the low warmth of a subwoofer and the air at the very top of a cymbal. We record real rooms, resynthesize their character, and fold the result back into something that feels less designed than remembered — a signal you swear you have heard before.

— The composers, RESONANCE
