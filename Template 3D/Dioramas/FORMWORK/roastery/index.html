02 — The journey

From cherry
to cup.

Great coffee is a chain of small, careful hours. Here's the road every bean travels before it reaches your grinder — scroll the track to follow it along.

01

Cherry

Hand-picked at peak ripeness, one deep-red cherry at a time — greens and unders left on the branch.

02

Ferment

Pulped and rested in tank, letting natural sugars break down over thirty-six patient hours.

03

Dry

Laid out thin on raised beds and turned by hand under the sun until the moisture is just right.

04

Roast

Roasted here in small drums over a low flame, each origin on its own curve to first crack and beyond.

05

Rest

Bagged with a one-way valve and left to degas for a few days, so the flavour settles and deepens.

06

Cup

Ground to order at your door and poured — the whole journey, finally, in a single warm cup.
